The harvest fights back.

For forty years, old Hank farmed Green Hollow in peace. Then Reactor Nine cracked, the coolant reached the irrigation canal, and the crops grew eyes. Then teeth. Then they stopped waiting to be harvested.

Farmer Survivors is an incremental survival action game. You only move: your scythe, pitchfork, bees and runaway tractor fight on their own. Mow through waves of radioactive vegetables, collect glowing crystals, choose your upgrades, and spend your coins on a farm that keeps earning while you are away.

What's inside

  • 3 fields, from Hank's back forty to the ruined Reactor Nine, each with a final boss.
  • 11 mutant vegetables that chase, spit, dash, explode and split apart.
  • 3 bosses: the Gourd King, the Corn Colossus and the Root of All Evil.
  • 9 weapons with evolved forms, and 10 passive items.
  • 4 playable farmers to unlock.
  • 16 permanent upgrades in the Workshop.
  • 6 idle buildings that earn coins even with the game closed.
  • Replant your farm for Clean Soil, a permanent bonus.
  • Radiation Levels: replay a field against tougher mutants for bigger payouts.
  • A short story, told in illustrated scenes and Hank's journal.

Starting tips

  • Keep moving. Circle the horde instead of running straight away from it.
  • Grab green XP chests: each gives a full level and pulls in every crystal on the map.
  • Max a weapon, own its matching item, and the next chest will evolve it.
  • Lost a run? Coins are kept. Spend them in the Workshop and go again.
  • To unlock the next field, defeat the boss that arrives at 8:00.

Controls

Move with WASD, arrow keys or a gamepad. Weapons fire on their own. Esc or P pauses.

On phones and tablets, turn your device sideways and drag anywhere on the screen to move. Tap the pause button in the corner to pause.

Languages

English, Português (Brasil), Español and Français. Change the language in Options.

FAQ

Does my progress save in the browser?
Yes, in your browser's storage on this device. Clearing site data erases it, and it is separate from the Windows version.

Windows warned me about the download.
The game is not code-signed, so Windows may show an unknown publisher warning.
